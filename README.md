# 📊 Data Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing company revenue using Python and SQL.  
The goal was to identify trends in sales and provide business recommendations.

---

## 🛠 Technologies Used
- Python
- SQLite
- Pandas
- Matplotlib

---

## 📈 Analysis Scope
The analysis covers company revenue in the period from January to March.

---

## 📊 Key Findings
- Revenue increased from **300 in January** to **800 in March**, indicating a strong upward trend.
- The growth was driven by a higher number of orders and increased customer activity in March.
- More than one customer contributed to revenue growth in the final period.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Analyze marketing or sales activities between February and March.
- Identify factors that contributed to increased customer engagement.
- Replicate successful strategies in future periods to maintain growth.
